# AIDB Packaging Prototype — "The New Jobs AI Will Create"

**Episode:** The New Jobs AI Will Create
**Aired:** May 11, 2026 · 7,950 views (as of submission)
**Link:** https://youtu.be/WhAAKxPlMhw
**Author:** Noel Santayana
**Built for:** AIDB Growth Engineer application

Two artifacts. One for peer-to-peer DM sharing. One for manager-to-team forwarding. Different audiences, different formats, same source episode.

---

## Artifact 1 — Peer card (for individual peer sharing)

**Format:** Single image card or screenshot-friendly text block. Built to be forwarded in DMs, texts, or quote-tweeted with one line of context.

**Visual concept:** Off-white background, AIDB serif headline, two-question split layout, seven-word footer list.

```
THE NEW JOBS AI WILL CREATE
NLW · The AI Daily Brief · May 11, 2026

The wrong question:
  "Can AI do the task?"

The right question:
  "Does AI-only delivery satisfy the demand?"

Seven things AI cannot underprice:

  Relationship · Embodied Presence · Trust
  Accountability · Translation · Behavior Change · Provenance

If your work delivers two or more, your premium is real.
If your work delivers none, redesign or get repriced.

Listen → [episode link]
```

**Designed for:** Founder DM-ing a founder. PT sending to a colleague. Lawyer sending to a partner. Single screenshot. No context required.

**Why it spreads:** The two-question reframe is the moment that makes someone think "I know who needs to see this." The seven-word list at the bottom is the defensibility tool — the receiver has vocabulary the next time they're pricing their work or defending their role.

## Artifact 2 — Team forward (for manager-to-team sharing)

**Format:** Email or Slack-channel post that a manager forwards to their team. Has a one-paragraph framing the manager can take credit for, plus the asset, plus a discussion prompt for the next team meeting.

```
Subject: 30-min listen, worth our next strategy meeting

Team —

I'd like everyone to listen to this before our offsite. NLW makes the most
useful argument I've heard for what our work becomes as AI does more of it.

The short version:

  - AI does not shrink demand. It expands it through six elasticities:
    price, access, complexity, continuity, personalization, and relational.

  - Where capacity opens up, new roles emerge:
    Navigators, Continuous Support Workers, AI-Augmented Service Operators,
    Data & Operations Specialists, QA & Safety, Escalation Specialists.

  - The human premium isn't going away. It's going up. In seven specific
    categories: relationship, embodied presence, trust, accountability,
    translation, behavior change, provenance.

I want us to come ready to discuss two questions:

  1. Where in our current work are we underpricing the human premium?
  2. Where are we still selling things AI can do for free?

Episode: [link]
Time: ~30 min
```

**Designed for:** COO, CMO, department head, clinical ops leader forwarding to their team. Comes pre-loaded with a framing the manager can claim. Sets up a real conversation, not a passive listen.

**Why it spreads:** The manager looks smart for sending it. The team gets a useful prompt for actual work. The episode embeds itself into the team's strategic vocabulary instead of getting a polite nod and a never-played status.

## What v2 would test

- **Format A vs B:** does the peer card outperform a 60-second NLW voice clip with the same crystallization?
- **Manager framing voice:** team-forward written as "the manager's take" vs "AIDB's summary the manager pastes in." Hypothesis: the first wins on send-rate, the second wins on listen-through.
- **Single-archetype variants:** does a healthcare-specific team forward outperform the generic one for clinical leaders? Same for legal, agency, etc.

These are week 3 work, not pre-submission work.
